A Sweet Home 3D alternative your AI can draw in

Sweet Home 3D taught a generation to draw their home: walls, rooms, furniture, a 3D view. 3D New Era AI keeps that way of working and adds a collaborator. Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or any other AI that speaks MCP can draw, furnish and render the plan with you. Every step shows up on screen, and you can undo any of them.

It is free and open source, and it opens the .sh3d projects you already have.

What carries over from Sweet Home 3D

  • Your projects. .sh3d files open with their walls, rooms, furniture, lights, cameras and levels.
  • The way of working. A plan and a live 3D view side by side, a furniture catalog, walls you draw with the mouse, doors and windows that snap into them.
  • Free, on your own computer. No account, no subscription. Windows, macOS and Linux, plus a browser editor with nothing to install.

What is new

  • An AI that works in your project. Describe a room, or attach a floor plan image, and the AI draws the walls, places doors and windows at real scale and furnishes each room with real dimensions. You watch it happen, and you take over at any point.
  • Made-to-measure joinery. Kitchens, wardrobes and slatted panels sized to the wall, with every board, sink cutout and appliance niche computed.
  • Reviews against real standards. Lighting per room following ISO/CIE 8995-1, ergonomics (space beside beds, in front of the stove, around tables), and blocked doors and colliding furniture, reported as a list you or the AI can fix.
  • Photoreal pictures. Path-traced photos with the sun at the hour you pick and the lamps you placed, even on a computer without a graphics card.
  • Four languages. English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.
