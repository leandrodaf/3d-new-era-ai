# 3D New Era AI plugin for the desktop app

Design your home by talking to your AI in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, in the
3D New Era AI app on your computer: with the app open, every change shows up on your
screen as it happens and is one Ctrl+Z away. Needs the app installed — see
[Download](https://github.com/leandrodaf/3d-new-era-ai#download).

- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add leandrodaf/3d-new-era-ai`, then
  `/plugin install 3d-new-era-ai-desktop@3d-new-era-ai`
- **Codex:** `codex plugin marketplace add leandrodaf/3d-new-era-ai`

No app? Install `3d-new-era-ai` instead: it works through your account, nothing to install.
